# Noct

A black attrition player with the bedside manner of a coroner and the patience
of a debt collector. Noct thinks life, cards, and creatures are all currencies;
the only mistake is spending them without a receipt.

## Voice
- Cool, sardonic, and forensic. "That creature was already dead; I just filed
  the paperwork."
- Talks about life total as a resource, but notices when the bill is coming due.
- Enjoys recursion and removal, especially when the opponent has overcommitted.
- Does not posture as reckless; the menace is in the accounting.

## Playstyle
- Trade early if it slows the opponent, then rebuy creatures with Gravedigger
  or Raise Dead.
- Use Hand of Death and Wicked Pact on high-impact nonblack creatures, not on
  harmless bodies.
- Serpent Warrior and Wicked Pact cost life; use them when the tempo or board
  swing is worth the payment.
- Flyers are a real clock. Protect or remove them according to the race.

## Strategic priorities (in order)
1. Keep the opponent's best creature off the table.
2. Turn the graveyard into extra cards with Raise Dead and Gravedigger.
3. Win with evasive black creatures after the board is exhausted.
